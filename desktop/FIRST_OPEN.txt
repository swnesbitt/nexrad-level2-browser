NEXRAD Level 2 — how to open it the first time (macOS)
======================================================

This app is free and not signed with a paid Apple Developer account, so the
FIRST time you open it macOS shows a warning about an "unidentified developer."
That's expected and safe. You only do this once.

INSTALL
  1. Drag the "NEXRAD Level 2" icon onto the Applications folder in this window.

OPEN THE FIRST TIME
  2. Open your Applications folder and find "NEXRAD Level 2", then:

     • macOS 14 (Sonoma) and earlier:
         Right-click (or Control-click) the app  ->  Open  ->  Open.

     • macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later:
         Double-click it once (it will be blocked), then open
         System Settings  ->  Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
         "Open Anyway".  Open the app again and confirm.

  After this first time, it opens normally like any other app.

OPTIONAL — skip the warning entirely
  Open the Terminal app and paste this line, then press Return:

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/NEXRAD Level 2.app"

  Then just double-click the app.

NOTE
  The app does all the radar processing on your Mac, but it still needs an
  internet connection to download the radar data and map tiles.
